**Subject:** Data license for a B2B prospecting product — redistribution + pricing

Hi PDL team,

I'm building **ProspectDaily** (prospectdaily.com), a B2B prospecting SaaS. Each
weekday we deliver our customers a set of net-new prospects matching their ideal
customer profile — company, contact name/title, verified work email, phone,
LinkedIn URL, plus a short fit rationale and a drafted intro email.

The prospect data would come from your Person and Company data, **embedded in our
product and delivered to our paying customers.** So my first and most important
question is about redistribution rights.

**Licensing**
1. Does our use — surfacing your person/company records (name, title, company,
   verified work email, phone, LinkedIn URL) inside a product we sell, delivered
   to our own B2B customers — fall within your standard license, or does it need
   a specific redistribution/OEM agreement? What are the terms and any per-record
   or downstream-use restrictions?
2. Any limits on how many end customers can receive a given record, or on the
   fields that may be passed downstream (e.g. is verified work email
   redistributable)?

**Data & cost**
3. For "find net-new B2B contacts matching an ICP (industry, title/seniority,
   company size, geography) each day, with verified work email," which
   endpoints do you recommend — Person Search, Person Enrichment, or a data
   license feed?
4. Is **verified work email** included in the base enrichment/search credit, or
   is it a premium field / separate call? Same question for **phone**.
5. What is the **per-record price** at our expected volume, and what volume/term
   commitment reaches your best rate?

**Volume (planning estimate — will refine):**
   ~3,000–8,000 enriched records/month to start, scaling toward 20,000+/month
   within 12 months as we add customers.

**Match rate & quality**
6. On a B2B ICP like ours, what typical **match rate** (share of searches
   returning a usable record *with* a verified work email) should we plan for?
   This drives our true cost per delivered lead.
7. Can we get a **trial / sandbox** (or a sample run against one of our real ICPs)
   to measure match rate and data quality before committing?

**Compliance**
8. How do you handle GDPR/CCPA, opt-outs, and suppression for contacts that end
   up receiving outreach — anything we need to pass through to our customers?

Happy to hop on a call. What's the best next step?
