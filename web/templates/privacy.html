Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

ProspectDaily ("we", "us") provides a B2B prospecting service that delivers business contact information and outreach content to our customers. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and password (stored hashed).
  • Targeting details — the answers you provide about your target audience, your company, and your outreach (used to find prospects and write intro emails).
  • Payment information — processed by our payment provider, Stripe. We do not store your full card details.
  • Google authorization — if you connect Google Drive, we receive a limited token that lets us create report files in a folder for you. We use the drive.file scope, which only allows access to files our app creates — never the rest of your Drive.
  • Usage data — basic logs (e.g., report runs) to operate and support the service.

Prospect (business contact) data

The prospects we deliver are business professionals' work contact details sourced from third-party B2B data providers. This data is provided to you for legitimate business-to-business outreach. You are responsible for using it in compliance with applicable laws (see our Terms).

How we use your information

To provide and improve the service: find and enrich prospects, generate intro emails, deliver daily reports, process billing, send service and onboarding emails, and provide support.

Service providers we share with

We share the minimum necessary data with providers that help us operate: People Data Labs (prospect data), Anthropic (AI email writing), Google (report delivery to your Drive), Stripe (payments), Resend (transactional email), and Render (hosting). We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention & security

We retain your account and delivery history while your account is active. Data is stored on secured, access-controlled infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your choices

You can update your targeting anytime, cancel your subscription, and request access to or deletion of your account data by emailing us.

Cookies

We use a single signed session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the date above.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@prospectdaily.com.
